## MTC MoveMint v1.1.14

- The updater can install an older signed release when the administrator enables rollback. The authorization is signed, short-lived, and specific to the device and version.
- A staged install rechecks the current update decision online before replacing the app. A canceled rollback will not install.
- Devices need v1.1.14 or a later compatible build before they can receive a rollback. Open the app to stage the selected release, then exit and reopen it to install.
