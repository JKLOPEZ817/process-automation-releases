## MTC MoveMint v1.1.18

- Tally Report defaults to the working-day export with one source prompt. Pasting exact DMS sales order IDs and selecting a date range remain available.
- Orders opens first, with filters and a frozen header. Order Qty, Ship Qty from SYSPRO, BackOrder Qty, and Qty UOM appear together. Available stock is visible, and supporting columns are collapsed.
- The app reads Excel and SYSPRO data in batches to reduce per-row processing.
- Download MTC_MoveMint.exe for manual installation. PROCESS_AUTOMATION.exe is provided for existing update clients.

The compiled workbook writer passed Excel validation with synthetic stock and order data. The packaged executable passed offline startup. Live SYSPRO was not tested.
