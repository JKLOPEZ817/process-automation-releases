## MTC MoveMint v1.1.17

- Tally Report checks each SKU against inventory in the warehouse on its sales order line. The Orders sheet highlights positive On Hand stock in green and zero or negative On Hand stock in red.
- Orders shows the warehouse, stocking unit, On Hand, Allocated, Available, Stock Free, and On Hand Status. Available and Stock Free are highlighted independently. If an inventory balance is missing, Orders shows Unknown.
- Download MTC_MoveMint.exe for manual installation. PROCESS_AUTOMATION.exe is provided for existing update clients.

The compiled workbook writer passed Excel highlight tests with simulated inventory. The packaged executable passed offline startup. Live SYSPRO was not tested.
