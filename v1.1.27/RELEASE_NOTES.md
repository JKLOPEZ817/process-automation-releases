## MTC MoveMint v1.1.27

- Orders resolves salesperson names even for sales orders outside the Ready To Print summaries. It uses the order branch's salesperson name when available, followed by a unique matching name for the code.
- The Salesperson column is wider so full names are easier to read.
- Download `MTC_MoveMint.exe` for manual installation. `PROCESS_AUTOMATION.exe` is the compatibility asset for existing update clients.

Verified with 30 tests, synthetic Excel data, and an offline startup check. Live SYSPRO and signed auto-update remain unverified.
