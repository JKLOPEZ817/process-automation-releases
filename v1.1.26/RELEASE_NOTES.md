## MTC MoveMint v1.1.26

- In automatic mode, Tally Report saves and refreshes the workbook in today's Pick List folder, even when the source export is from an earlier day.
- Use **D MMDD** to set the output day explicitly.
- Download `MTC_MoveMint.exe` for manual installation. `PROCESS_AUTOMATION.exe` is the compatibility asset for existing update clients.

Verified with 28 tests, a synthetic Excel workbook saved and refreshed in the current-day folder, and an offline startup check. Live SYSPRO and signed auto-update remain unverified.
