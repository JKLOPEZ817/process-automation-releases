## MTC MoveMint v1.1.7

- The Loadsheet hides both rows of repeated customer headings while preserving the first heading, customer details, and invoice rows.
- The main menu shows the running version. Routine licensing success and lease expiration messages stay out of the console.
- The updater removes its previous executable backup after a successful upgrade.

The Loadsheet change was verified in desktop Excel against 12 exports. Updater backup cleanup was verified with a test executable.
